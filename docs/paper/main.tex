\begin{abstract}
  % TEK paragraf, <=300 kelime. Dort oge zorunlu:
  %   1) calismanin amaci ve arastirilan problem
  %   2) arastirma yaklasiminin temel tasarimi
  %   3) analizin basli ca bulgulari
  %   4) yorum ve sonuclarin kisa ozeti
  % En sona yazilacak; simdilik iskelet.
  Taxi-out time---the interval between off-block and take-off---is a primary driver of
  excess fuel burn on the airport surface, yet it is both hard to observe and hard to
  predict. [AMAC] This study predicts taxi-out time for departures at eleven major
  European airports using one full year of movement records. [TASARIM] We decompose the
  problem following EUROCONTROL's own additional taxi-out time indicator, reproducing its
  reference component and learning the residual with gradient boosting over congestion,
  routing and meteorological features. [BULGULAR] [SAYI: RMSE, en iyi yapilandirma,
  aile katkilari] [YORUM] We further separate a retrospective model, appropriate for
  post-operations analysis, from a causal one usable in real time, and quantify the
  information value of retrospective observability. [SAYI: iki modelin farki]
\end{abstract}
